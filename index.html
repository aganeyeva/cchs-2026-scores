Where the offer line fell, across the years

CCHS has published the anonymised scores for three intakes — 1,048 candidates for September 2020, 1,225 for September 2025 and 1,198 for September 2026 — together with the score ranges of the last girl offered a place in each year. The lines move a point or two every year, and Samira's 106.68 (September 2027 entry) clears the 2020 line outright, sits inside the 2026 range, and misses the 2025 one. Samira's 2027 cohort, against the record

The 2027 figures come from the school's results email, not a published score list — the full anonymised results for Samira's cohort won't appear until spring 2027. Set against the published years, the picture is mixed, and three discrepancies matter.

−1 pt vs +2 pts

The lines moved in opposite directions. From 2025 to 2026 the priority last-offered range eased a point (107–108 → 106–107) while the outside range tightened two points (116–117 → 118–119). There is no single “CCHS line” — the two categories drift independently.

96 → 77

Outside clearances fell as the standard rose. More outside girls met the writing standard in 2026 (125, up from 111), yet fewer cleared the line — the clearance rate dropped from 86.5% to 61.6%. The two-point rise in the outside line did all of that.

476 eligible

Samira's cohort looks bigger at the gate. The 2027 email counts 476 eligible candidates — more than the closest equivalent in either published year (417 in 2025 and 443 in 2026, counting “Of the Standard” girls scoring 92+). If the measures match, her year is the most competitive of the three at the eligibility stage; the email's exact definition may differ, so read it as a signal, not a statistic.

What this does — and doesn't — decide

“Clearing the academic line” is not the same as receiving an offer. In 2020, 277 girls cleared it, in 2025, 244 and in 2026, 228 — but many candidates never name CCHS on their application form at all, and places are allocated by the admissions policy and preferences — not by this count alone.

Read the numbers with these caveats

  • Different year, different line. The year panels describe the September 2020, 2025 and 2026 intakes; the 2027 figures come from the results email alone. Samira is applying for September 2027; the thresholds will move with that cohort's scores.
  • “Passed” means academically over the line — score plus writing standard against the last-offered ranges CCHS disclosed. It is not a count of offers made.
  • Area is decided by the school's own measurement of the home-to-school distance against the 12.5-mile priority boundary (Policy 1.5), not by a map estimate.
  • The writing standard is a separate gate. A candidate needs both the score and “Of the Standard” in creative writing — as the 3 girls at 118+ who missed it show.
