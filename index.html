Where the catchment offer line fell, across the years

This page now follows one group only: girls inside the 12.5-mile priority (catchment) area — Samira's category. Of everyone who sat the test, 559 catchment candidates sat for September 2019, 514 for September 2020, 559 for September 2025 and 543 for September 2026. In each year the funnel runs the same way: catchment candidates who sat the test → those who met the year's bar with the writing standard → those who cleared the catchment offer line. Samira's 106.68 (September 2027 entry) clears the 2020 line outright, sits inside the 2026 range, and misses the 2025 one. Switch years with the tabs below; the final section compares all five cohorts.

Samira's 2027 cohort, against the record

The 2027 figures come from the school's results email, not a published score list — the full anonymised results for Samira's cohort won't appear until spring 2027. The figures below are catchment-only, set against the published years — one exception is labelled where it occurs: the 476-eligible comparison, which is all-areas because no catchment split exists for 2027.

107–108 → 106–107

The catchment line eased a point. From 2025 to 2026 the catchment last-offered range fell from 107–108 to 106–107. That single point is the difference between Samira's 106.68 falling short (on 2025's line) and sitting inside the range (on 2026's).

148 → 151

Catchment clearances barely moved. Despite the lower line, only three more catchment girls cleared it in 2026 than in 2025 (151 vs 148) — the 2026 catchment cohort's top end was thinner, which is exactly why the line fell.

476 eligible

Samira's cohort looks bigger at the gate. The 2027 email counts 476 eligible candidates — an all-areas figure, so it is compared like-for-like with the all-areas equivalents in the published years (417 in 2025 and 443 in 2026, counting “Of the Standard” girls scoring 92+). If the measures match, her year is the most competitive of the three at the eligibility stage; the email's exact definition may differ, so read it as a signal, not a statistic.

What this does — and doesn't — decide

“Clearing the academic line” is not the same as receiving an offer. Inside the catchment, 152 girls cleared it in 2019, 165 in 2020, 148 in 2025 and 151 in 2026 — but many candidates never name CCHS on their application form at all, and places are allocated by the admissions policy and preferences — not by this count alone.

Read the numbers with these caveats

  • Different year, different line. The year panels describe the September 2019, 2020, 2025 and 2026 intakes; the 2027 figures come from the results email alone. Samira is applying for September 2027; the thresholds will move with that cohort's scores.
  • “Passed” means academically over the line — score plus writing standard against the last-offered ranges CCHS disclosed. It is not a count of offers made.
  • Area is decided by the school's own measurement of the home-to-school distance against the 12.5-mile priority boundary (Policy 1.5), not by a map estimate.
  • The writing standard is a separate gate. A catchment candidate needs both the score and “Of the Standard” in creative writing — in 2026, 410 catchment girls met the standard, but only 365 of them also cleared the 87-point bar, and only 151 the offer line.
