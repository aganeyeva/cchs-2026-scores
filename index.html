What this does — and doesn't — decide

“Clearing the academic line” is not the same as receiving an offer. In 2026, 228 girls cleared it, but many candidates never name CCHS on their application form at all, and places are allocated by the admissions policy and preferences — not by this count alone.

Read the numbers with these caveats

  • Different year, different line. Every figure above describes the September 2026 intake. Samira is applying for September 2027; the thresholds will move with that cohort's scores.
  • “Passed” means academically over the line — score plus writing standard against the last-offered ranges CCHS disclosed. It is not a count of offers made.
  • Area is decided by the school's own measurement of the home-to-school distance against the 12.5-mile priority boundary (Policy 1.5), not by a map estimate.
  • The writing standard is a separate gate. A candidate needs both the score and “Of the Standard” in creative writing — as the 3 girls at 118+ who missed it show.
